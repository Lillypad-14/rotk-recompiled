ROTK Recompiled {version} (preview)
================================

A native PC version of The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (GBA),
with the ROTK Expanded mod built in.

You need your own files (none are included):
  - The Return of the King, GBA, USA/Europe ROM (SHA-1 ec3d126d37ea6e2ad7030d8a99cf87c6e46c03fd)
  - A GBA BIOS dump (SHA-1 300c20df6731a33952ded8c436f7f186d25d3492)
  - Optional: The Two Towers (GBA, USA/Europe) ROM, and its .sav for your heroes

How to play
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere you can write to. Updating an older download?
     Delete RotkPcLab.exe and RotkPcLabExpanded.exe if they are there (the old names).
  2. Run ROTK-Recompiled.exe. The launcher opens. (ROTK-Recompiled-Expanded.exe
     is started by it when Expanded is ticked; you don't run it yourself.)
  3. On the front page, press Browse For ROM and pick your ROTK ROM, then
     Browse For BIOS and pick your GBA BIOS. Play unlocks once both verify.
  4. Tick "Expanded" on the front page for the Expanded mod, or leave it off
     for the original game, and press Play. The first Expanded start makes a
     patched copy of your ROM beside it (your original is never changed).

The Two Towers (Expanded only)
  Put your Two Towers ROM (and its .sav) in the same folder as your ROTK ROM.
  - Import a hero: Load Game menu, press L on an empty slot.
  - Skill page 3: Two Towers skills for Frodo, Legolas, Aragorn, Gandalf,
    Eowyn and Gimli, bought with skill points. Work in progress.
  - Mod Options > Two Towers > Legacy Skills turns them off.

Settings: the launcher's Mods page (HD audio, HD icons, smooth motion,
widescreen, split-screen co-op and more).
